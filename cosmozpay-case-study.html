Featured Case Study

COSMOZPAY

COSMOZPAY is a bill-payment platform designed to simplify the way people access essential digital services such as data purchases, airtime, electricity, cable subscriptions, and education payments.

Role

Product Design, User Experience, Digital Service Design

Focus

Clarity, trust, speed, and a smoother payments experience

Overview

Designing a digital service around everyday essentials.

Problem

People needed a simpler, more trustworthy way to handle recurring utility and digital transactions without friction or confusion.

Opportunity

The product needed to feel immediate, accessible, and premium while remaining practical for frequent, high-volume actions.

Objectives

Improve clarity, reduce decision friction, support diverse service categories, and define a cohesive digital brand experience.

Deliverables

Interface design, mobile-first flows, service structure, and product design direction across the user journey.

Design Approach

Thoughtful UX decisions that make complex actions feel simple.

01 — Discovery

Mapped essential user needs, service categories, and points of friction that affected confidence and completion.

02 — Flow Design

Built clear user journeys with stronger hierarchy, readable transaction steps, and a calmer mobile-first rhythm.

03 — Visual System

Created a clean interface language that communicates trust, speed, and utility without feeling overly technical or crowded.

Interface Highlights

Product design for speed, clarity, and everyday trust.
